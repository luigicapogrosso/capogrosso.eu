About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Interdisciplinary Transformation University of Austria (IT:U), working with Prof. Michele Magno.

I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence (cum laude) from the Polytechnic of Turin, in collaboration with the University of Verona, under the supervision of Prof. Marco Cristani and Prof. Franco Fummi. During my Ph.D. I was a visiting scholar at the Instituto Superior Técnico, working with Prof. Mário A. T. Figueiredo. Before that, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science (2019) and my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering for Robotics and Smart Industry (2021) from the University of Verona.

I am a member of the Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica (GNCS), IEEE, and the Computer Vision Foundation (CVF).
